    Electrified Family Adventure​ Unplugged. Unlimited.​

    Meet Nissan’s family adventure SUV, powered by e-POWER for a smooth and quiet EV-like driving experience, without the need for external plug-in charging. With e-4ORCE all-wheel control technology, the X-TRAIL delivers confident control and a uniquely electrified drive.<br> With flexible seating for up to seven, there’s ample room for the whole family and everything that comes along for the journey. Advanced Nissan Intelligent Mobility technologies help keep everyone safe and comfortable, while cutting-edge connectivity keeps passengers entertained along the way.<br> From everyday journeys to weekend getaways, the X-TRAIL makes every family adventure more enjoyable.

    Strongest e-POWER System EVER

    Composed of a fuel-efficient turbocharged petrol engine and lithium-ion battery working together to power an electric motor, e-POWER delivers a unique, responsive, and quiet driving experience, without the need to plug in for recharging. Available with e-4ORCE all-wheel control technology, the X-TRAIL delivers enhanced power, confident traction, and stable handling across a variety of driving conditions. Together, these technologies deliver an EV-like driving experience that is ready for every journey, from highway cruising and urban traffic to off-road terrain.

    e-4ORCE – revolutionary all-wheel control technology

    Take on new terrains and push further with the X-TRAIL’s e-4ORCE technology.<br> Combining Nissan’s 100% electric propulsion, all-wheel control, and chassis-control technologies, e-4ORCE delivers confident acceleration, cornering, and braking across every adventure. With five selectable drive and terrain modes — Auto, Sport, Eco, OffRoad and Snow, get ready to explore wherever the journey takes you.
