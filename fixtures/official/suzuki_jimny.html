DESCRIPTION

The Jimny is designed for the road (and the off-road) less travelled. Fully loaded with a 1.5L engine, ALLGRIP PRO 4WD system, and 4 Speed Auto gearbox. It’s bold. It’s rugged. It’s outdoorsy. Wherever you’re headed, the Jimny can get you there.

PERFORMANCE

The Jimny’s K15B engine is designed with adventure in mind. Lightweight and compact, it’s capable of delivering plenty of torque in low revs—perfect when roads become tracks. And when you’re ready to mix it with the city slickers again, the Jimny delivers—without sacrificing fuel efficiency.

EXTERIOR

The Jimny got buff! Muscular wheel arches and LED headlamps make the Jimny look big and bold, just like previous generations of classic off-roaders. It’s also available in 8 distinctive colours, including monotone and two-tone styles.
*Image for illustration purpose only. Local specs may vary.

INTERIOR

Want proof you can be humble and handsome? Look no further than the Jimny’s interior. It’s stylish and simple—perfect for situations where mud comes aboard as often as passengers. The Jimny puts also everything you need within easy reach, with controls that were designed to be easy to operate—even in gloves!
*Image for illustration purpose only. Local specs may vary

ADVANCED SAFETY

Whether you’re on the road or off it, safety matters. The Jimny’s features help keep you and your passengers safe, including Dual Sensor Brake Support (DSBS), vehicle sway warning, 6 airbags, and hill hold and hill descent control.
