       SAFETY

      Forward Collision Avoidance Assist (FCW & AEB)

      Using advanced sensors, the system monitors the road ahead and warns the driver if a potential collision is detected. If the driver does not respond in time, Autonomous Emergency Braking automatically applies the brakes to help avoid or reduce the impact of a collision.

      Lane Departure Warning & Lane Keep Assist (LDW & LKA)

      Cameras monitor the vehicle’s position within the lane and alert the driver if it begins to drift unintentionally. Lane Keep Assist can then gently adjust the steering to help guide the vehicle back into the lane.

      Rear Cross Traffic Alert (RCTA)

      Employs sensors to detect vehicles or pedestrians crossing behind the vehicle while reversing.

      360 Degree Panoramic Camera

      This camera system offers a comprehensive view around the vehicle, enhancing visibility and making parking and maneuvering in tight spaces easier and safer.
