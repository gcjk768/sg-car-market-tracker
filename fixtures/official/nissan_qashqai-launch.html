Confidence in every moment

The Nissan QASHQAI offers all-around protection for total peace of mind. Designed to anticipate and respond to every situation, it provides advanced features that keep you and your passengers safe and confident on any journey.

DISCOVER MORE

1/2 Intelligent Cruise Control

The QASHQAI's system automatically adjusts to the car ahead, maintaining a safe distance and adapting to changing speeds—easing the driver’s burden and even bringing the car to a complete stop when needed.

1/2 Intelligent Emergency Braking With Forward Collision Warning

Intelligent Emergency Braking with Forward Collision Warning scans for obstacles ahead, alerting the driver to act. If necessary, it automatically applies the brakes to reduce damage or avoid collisions, ensuring safer, more confident drives.

1/2 Rear Cross Traffic Alert

When you're backing out of your space, rear cross traffic alert can warn you about cars it detects that are approaching from either side.

1/2 Intelligent Blind Spot Warning

Blind spot warning can give you a heads-up if it detects a vehicle hiding in your blind spot.

1/2 Rear Automatic Emergency Braking

Back up with increased confidence. If a stationary item is detected behind your vehicle, the system can automatically engage the brakes to help avoid a collision.

1/2 Intelligent Around-View Monitor with Moving Object Detection

Qashqai's Intelligent Around-View Monitor with Moving Object Detection offers a 360° view and alerts you to nearby movement, ensuring safer and easier parking.
