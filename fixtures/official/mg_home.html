MG SINGAPORE

An Iconic British Brand

MG (Morris Garages), has been completely reimagined for a new generation of motorists. Founded in 1924 and renowned for its sports cars – including the legendary two-seater Midget – MG is synonymous with affordable fun for drivers seeking fully electric powered vehicles including SUVs and wagons, as well as conventionally powered models.
