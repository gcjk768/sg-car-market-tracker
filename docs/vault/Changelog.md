* Fix: Sgcarmart's used petrol results page missed the 30 second load limit on every run on 2026-10-01 (and the EV page on 3 of 4). Headless Chromium now skips images, fonts and media, which the scraper never reads, and rendered pages get their own `general.render_timeout_seconds` (90) (`scrapers/base.py` `fetch_rendered`). Live on the NAS the page that failed rendered in about 6 seconds after Sgcarmart's 30 second crawl delay.
* New Motorbikes section on request, after Top sellers (`report.py` `motorbike_section`, `scrapers/motorbikes.py`, `config.yaml` `motorbikes`). Used bikes per licence class (2B up to 200cc, 2A 201 to 400cc, 2 above 400cc) from SGBikemart's result cards (robots.txt allows all; no detail pages, 3 class searches of up to 3 pages), filtered by COE left (10 years from registration) and mileage, lowest depreciation per year first, ⚡ for electric. Top selling bike brands from LTA table M04 (new motorcycle registrations by make and fuel). Cat D now shows in the COE section (`coe.display_categories`). New bike prices are not listed: SGBikemart's model pages carry none and dealer ads mix full prices, deposits and instalments. Live run: 30 ads per class, 8 shown, 2 messages.
* NAS vault, movement log and memory (the NAS app vault standard). New `vault.py`, on when `VAULT_DIR` is set (compose mounts `/volume1/<USER>/Obsidian/SG Car Market` at `/vault`). It writes `Activity/YYYY-MM-DD.md`, one line per event in SGT (`- HH:MM emoji **what** · detail · [[note]]`: run started and finished, each scraper's counts, new and gone listings, price drops and rises, COE results, cars reported, /run /coe /filters /ask, self repairs), one note per tracked used listing in `Cars/` (`Make Model year — source id`, price History, archived when gone, with days on market) and one per COE category in `COE/` (tender History once per tender). Notes are written atomically, 664 and owned like the vault folder. Read side: the analyst note and `/ask` prompts get a capped excerpt (`vault.memory`, 4,000 chars, newest first) of the relevant car notes and the last 7 days of Activity, so the model sees price trends and days on market (`ai.py` `with_memory`). A car the vault shows was already reported at its price is not logged or offered to the analyst note as news again (`vault.already_reported`, `cars_reported`); a new price makes it news again. The Telegram report text is unchanged: the vault never touches `report.py` or the section HTML (test `test_report_html_is_the_same_with_the_vault_on`). Every vault call is best effort and logs instead of raising. `tests/conftest.py` clears `VAULT_DIR` so the suite, which `heal.py` runs inside the container, never writes to the live vault.
* `.dockerignore` now leaves out all of `data/`. The root only `data/claude/sessions` folder made the image build fail, and nothing in `data/` belongs in the image (it is bind mounted).
* Fix: vans and lorries filled the used lists and the cost table (Toyota Town Ace as "Cheapest to own", Dyna, Honda N-Van, Suzuki Every in the EV list, a Skoda Octavia mild hybrid as an EV). Motorist detail pages have no vehicle type, so `exclude_vehicle_types` never fired there; goods vehicles are now also caught by model name, whole words in the title (`used.filters.exclude_models`, `scrapers/used_common.py` `is_commercial_model`). And `detect_drivetrain` matched the EV word " ev" inside "Every", and fell back to the search's default (EV) when the page said Petrol: "ev" is now a whole word, Petrol or Diesel means ICE, and MHEV and Petrol-Electric mean hybrid. Tests from the live titles in `tests/test_vehicle_kind.py`.
* Top sellers now lists every EV model of every brand on request, not one best seller: `top_sellers.ev_models` takes a list per LTA make, best to least selling (43 brands, 113 models, researched 2026-10-02 from news, distributor releases and MotorMetrics, since no model sales are published; least certain brands named in the config comment). One block per brand, one line per model with price, deposit and monthly instalment when it is on today's price list (`report.py` `top_sellers_section`, `scrapers/new_ev.py` `lineup`, `model_prices` now {make: {model: price}}). On today's data 37 models are priced, 2 messages.

## 2026-09-30

* Top sellers in SG: the Top EV brands list now shows up to 50 brands on request (`top_sellers.top_n_ev`), the petrol list keeps `top_n` at 20 (`report.py` `top_sellers_section`).
* Fix: used car runs spent the whole AI budget (20 calls) and priced sold Motorist cars wrongly. Replaying the day's cached live pages showed three causes. Motorist keeps sold cars in its results; a sold detail page has no asking price, so the unlabelled price fallback read the COE premium as the price and road tax as depreciation, then asked the model for the blank mileage. Sold cards are now skipped before opening (`SOLD` on the card) and refused on the detail page (`sold_marker` in `scrapers/used_motorist.py`), and the unlabelled price and depreciation are read only between the title and the spec table (`scrapers/used_common.py` `parse_detail`). Sold cards also climbed into the whole results list looking for a price and all took one neighbour's text, so `_card_container` now stops before a parent that holds another listing. A field the page labels but leaves blank (Sgcarmart "Mileage N.A.") no longer triggers the AI fallback, since the model cannot find it either. Replayed, both sites now parse every active listing with no AI calls.
* Fix: searches only reached cars the filters reject. With `min_coe_years_remaining: 6` on a 10 year COE only cars registered in the last four years qualify, but the URLs asked for 2018 and 2020 onwards, and Sgcarmart's petrol search had no price or year filter at all (1 car opened per run). Search URLs now take `{price_max}` and `{year_min}`, filled from the filters (`used.filters.coe_term_years`, new), and Sgcarmart uses its `pr2` and `fr` parameters, checked on the live site.
* `scrapers/base.py`: a 403 is no longer retried. It is an anti bot block (Tesla answered three times a run), and asking again is trying to get past it.
* Fix: Best Selling Top EV found no rows on the live DataMall spreadsheet. Its header is one cell, `Make/ Importer Type/ Fuel Type/ Car Type`, so the fuel column was taken as the make column and every row was skipped. A fuel header in the make column now falls back to the last text cell (`scrapers/registrations.py` `parse_body_types_rows`, test with the live header).
* Resilience and self repair on request. `scheduler.py` is now a supervisor: reports and the listener run as child processes (reports stopped after `resilience.max_run_minutes` with their Chromium), the listener restarts with backoff, failed or degraded runs retry after `retry_after_minutes`, a watchdog exits for a clean restart if the loop stalls, `data/heartbeat` feeds a Docker healthcheck, missed runs catch up on start, old cache and repair folders are pruned daily, logs rotate. New `heal.py`: after a second failure it runs `claude -p` with an allow list (edit `scrapers/`, add under `fixtures/` and `tests/`, run pytest and dry runs, no git), then keeps the change only if it stays in bounds, the tests pass and the failed sections recover, else restores every file; each attempt is recorded under `data/self_heal/` and reported on Telegram. `main.py` writes `last_run_health` for both. Compose: `init: true`, healthcheck, `mem_limit` 2g. A live rehearsal with a planted COE parser bug was diagnosed, fixed and kept for $0.17. Old branch `claude/upbeat-darwin-qdpff5` confirmed gone, `main` is the default.
* Summary pick relabelled "Cheapest to own today" (was "Best value today"), since it is the car with the lowest estimated yearly cost and the lists are now Best Selling lists (`report.py` `summary_section`).
* Phone preview of the Telegram report: `scripts/telegram_preview.py` renders the sample report's exact HTML as a Telegram style chat 390 pixels wide, one PNG per group of sections, into `docs/telegram_preview/`. The README shows the four images. Regenerate after changing `report.py`.
* Architecture diagram updated on request: new LTA DataMall source (M03 spreadsheet, EV sales by brand and body type) with Carro moved down a slot, Best Selling ordering in `pipeline.py`, car cards in `report.py`, `/ask` on `bot_listener.py` with a dashed link to the Claude CLI, whose box now names the listing fallback, analyst note and `/ask`. The same edits were applied to draw.io's SVG export (changed labels drop their fallback pictures, the embedded editable copy is the new source) and the PNG was rendered from that SVG with Chromium, because draw.io itself cannot be reached from the build environment. Opening and re-exporting `docs/architecture.drawio` in draw.io gives the same picture.
* Used lists renamed on request to Best Selling Used EV and Best Selling Used Petrol Car, and ordered to match: cars that pass the filters come in order of their brand's new registrations this year (electric for the EV list, petrol and petrol hybrid for the other, LTA table M03), then by lowest depreciation within a brand. Each card names its brand's rank. No used car sales figures are published, so new car sales stand in for brand popularity, and the section note says so (`filters.py` `shortlist` `brand_sales`, `report.py` `used_section`, `pipeline.py` `registrations`, which now reads the PDF once per run for both uses). `used.rank_by: value` restores the Best Value lists, which are also the fallback when the table cannot be read. The cost comparison's used picks follow the new order.
* New EV Car Best Value list replaced by Best Selling Top EV on request. For each body type, brands are ranked by this year's new electric car registrations and each brand lists its models of that body type from today's price list, cheapest first, with price, deposit, instalment and depreciation, and no value score (`report.py` `best_selling_ev_section`, `scrapers/new_ev.py` `best_selling_by_body_type`). The body type split comes from the spreadsheet copy of LTA table M03 on DataMall, because the PDF's extracted text drops empty cells and loses which column a count belongs to (`scrapers/registrations.py` `parse_body_types_xlsx`, new `openpyxl` dependency). The cost comparison's new EV is now the best seller. `new_ev.rank_by: value` restores the old list, and it is also the fallback when the spreadsheet cannot be read. Used car lists keep their Best Value order.
* Top sellers in SG split into Top EV brands and Top petrol brands on request, each ranked by that fuel's new registrations with its share. Petrol counts petrol and petrol hybrid rows, since pure petrol is only 2,004 of 13,615 (`scrapers/registrations.py` `petrol` count, `report.py` `top_sellers_section`).
* New `/ask <question>` Telegram command (`bot_listener.py` `ask_text`). Answers from the last full report plus general knowledge through the Claude CLI, so it needs the CLI signed in on the NAS.

* `scrapers/base.py`: only transient errors are retried (network, 429, 5xx; 403 dropped later the same day), via `_transient`. A 404 was fetched three times. `scrapers/used_common.py` logs a 404 past page 1 as the normal end of Motorist results, not a warning.
* Docs: README rewritten (highlights, flow, stack, limitations) and a new draw.io architecture diagram, `docs/architecture.drawio` with `docs/architecture.drawio.svg` and `docs/architecture.png` exports.
* Telegram layout made phone friendly on request. The 60 character `<pre>` tables wrapped on a phone and every car was listed twice (table row, then a numbered link). Pump prices and cost of ownership tables are 32 and 36 wide (`telegram.table_width` 60 to 36). Method notes are collapsed quotes. `split_message` splits on blank lines first.

The real limit was the candidate pool (70 used EVs gave 4 that passed the filters), so Motorist searches now pre filter by `price_max` and `year_min`, and `max_list_pages` 3 to 5, `max_detail_pages_per_search` 40 to 80.
